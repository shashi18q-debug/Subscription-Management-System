# Subscription Management System

The Subscription Management System is a centralized platform designed to help users efficiently manage, monitor, and organize all their subscriptions in one place. From entertainment and streaming services to software, productivity tools, and other recurring payments, the system provides a convenient way to keep track of subscription details and expenses.

The platform helps users monitor subscription costs, track billing cycles, manage renewal dates, and stay informed about upcoming payments. By providing a clear overview of active subscriptions and recurring expenses, it makes budgeting easier and helps prevent unexpected charges or forgotten renewals.

### Key Features
- **Subscription Tracking:** Add, update, and manage all subscriptions from a single dashboard.
- **Payment Monitoring:** Keep track of subscription costs and recurring payments.
- **Renewal Reminders:** Stay informed about upcoming billing dates and renewals.
- **Expense Overview:** Monitor monthly and yearly subscription spending.
- **Category Management:** Organize subscriptions into categories such as Entertainment, Software, Education, and Productivity.
- **Subscription Status:** Easily identify active, expired, and cancelled subscriptions.

### Objective
The main goal of this project is to simplify subscription management, improve financial awareness, and give users better control over their recurring expenses through an organized and user-friendly interface.
